Study Spot Recommendations

Grainger Engineering Library

A pretty popular spot, but for a reason - it's open 24/7 Monday through Friday and has plenty of seating options on both quiet and collaborative floors. Once classes ramp up, it gets hard to find space though.

Department of Psychology

Silent and ambient. There's an Espresso Royale across the street too!

Krannert Center for the Performing Arts

Very underrated! Plenty of seating and a cafe open throughout the day. It's right across from ISR (great for freshmen dorming there) and light jazz adds to the ambiance.
